Use cases

How teams use Edgebric

HR stops answering repeat questions

Scenario: HR admin onboards a new hire

Upload the employee handbook, benefits guide, and PTO policy

Drag files into a “Company Policies” data source. Edgebric indexes everything automatically.

New hire asks: “How many PTO days do I get in my first year?”

They open Edgebric in their browser—no software to install. Just a link on the company network.

Instant answer citing page 14 of the employee handbook

The new hire gets a clear answer with the exact source. No waiting for HR to respond. No one knows they asked.

HR stops answering the same 20 questions every time someone new joins. New team members get private, instant answers from day one.

The team stays aligned without more meetings

Scenario: Product and engineering teams sharing specs

Sync specs from Google Drive into Edgebric

Connect the team’s shared Drive folder. Edgebric stays in sync as documents are added or updated.

“Summarize the auth redesign spec”

An engineer asks during sprint planning. Edgebric pulls key points from the 12-page spec with page references.

Everyone stays on the same page

New team members catch up on past decisions. Anyone can query specs, RFCs, and design docs without scheduling a meeting.

No more “did you read the spec?” meetings. The spec is always one question away.

Reps self-serve instead of pinging product

Scenario: Sales rep preparing for a competitive call

Upload sales playbooks, battlecards, and competitive intel

Sales ops drops in the latest materials. Product adds technical differentiators. Edgebric indexes everything.

“What differentiates us from Competitor X on latency?”

Before a call, the rep asks Edgebric. Gets a cited answer pulled directly from the battlecard and product spec.

Rep walks into the call prepared

No last-minute Slack messages to product. No waiting for someone to dig up the right document. Self-service, cited answers.

Reps self-serve instead of pinging product. Product stops being an on-demand FAQ hotline.

Institutional knowledge, accessible to everyone

Scenario: Company-wide knowledge base across departments

Upload policies, meeting notes, contracts, and memos

Each department adds their documents. Legal uploads contracts, ops uploads policies, leadership uploads meeting notes.

“What did we agree to with Acme in Q3?”

Edgebric searches contracts, meeting notes, and emails at once. Returns the relevant clause with the exact source document.

Everyone finds answers without asking around

No more “does anyone know where that doc is?” messages. No more knowledge locked in one person’s inbox or head.

Institutional knowledge accessible to everyone, not locked in someone’s inbox.

Built for teams

Why teams choose Edgebric

Your whole team, one install

Install Edgebric on one machine. Everyone else opens it in their browser. No software to deploy, no per-seat licenses.

Group chats with AI built in

Create group conversations and tag the AI to pull answers from shared data. Threaded replies keep discussions organized.

Every answer cites its source

See exactly which file and page an answer came from. Your team can verify before acting on it.

Your data never leaves your network

Everything runs on your hardware. No cloud processing, no third-party servers. Sensitive data stays where it belongs.

Syncs with your existing storage

Connect Google Drive or OneDrive. Edgebric auto-syncs when files change. No manual uploading.

Role-based access control

Admins manage who sees what. Control access per data source so each team only sees the documents they should.

Vault Mode for personal privacy

Even on a shared team setup, anyone can create encrypted personal data sources on their own device. Private notes, sensitive files—invisible to admins and other team members.

Getting started

Up and running in minutes

1. Install the app

Download, drag to Applications, double-click. Edgebric downloads and manages the AI for you automatically. No technical setup.

2. Add your data

Connect Google Drive, OneDrive, or other storage — Edgebric syncs automatically. You can also drag and drop files directly.

+ more models

3. Ask your AI anything

Ask questions, get answers with citations. Summarize files, search across everything — all powered by AI running on your own hardware.
