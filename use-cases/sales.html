Use case

Edgebric for Sales & Product

Your sales team gets instant answers from product specs, playbooks, and competitive intel. Product sees what questions keep coming up. All without data leaving your network.

Workflows

How sales and product teams use Edgebric

From pre-call prep to closing the feedback loop between field and product.

Pre-call prep in minutes

Scenario: Account executive preparing for a prospect call

Upload battlecards, call scripts, and competitive intel

Drag battlecards and call scripts into a “Sales Enablement” data source. Edgebric indexes everything automatically.

“What’s our response to the pricing objection?”

Edgebric returns the answer directly from the playbook, citing the exact section and page number.

Walk into the call ready

Objection handling, competitor positioning, pricing guidance—all at your fingertips without digging through shared drives.

Reps prep in minutes, not hours. Every answer is grounded in your actual playbook with citations you can verify.

Sales self-serves without pinging engineering

Scenario: Sales rep needs technical details before a demo

Product specs and sales playbooks live in shared data sources

Product uploads specs, release notes, and architecture docs. Sales uploads playbooks and battlecards. Both indexed together.

“What’s the difference between our Standard and Pro tiers on API rate limits?”

Edgebric searches across product specs and returns the answer with a citation to the exact spec document.

No Slack messages to engineers, no waiting for answers

The rep gets what they need instantly. Engineering stays focused. The answer cites the source so it’s trustworthy.

Sales self-serves without pinging engineering. Technical details are always up to date because Edgebric re-indexes when specs change.

Real-time collaboration with AI in the loop

Scenario: Sales team preparing for a competitive deal

Start a group chat and share relevant data sources

Create a group chat for the deal. Attach the competitive intel and product spec data sources so everyone has context.

Tag @edgebric: “What differentiates us from Competitor X on latency?”

The AI pulls the answer from the competitive battlecard and product spec, citing both sources in the thread.

Team discusses the AI’s answer in the thread

Reps add their own field experience. Product chimes in with context. The AI answers follow-up questions as the discussion evolves.

Real-time collaboration with AI in the loop. The whole team builds on each other’s knowledge in one threaded conversation.

Close the feedback loop between field and product

Scenario: Product manager reviewing what sales keeps asking about

Product reviews what reps are asking

Browse the questions sales has been asking Edgebric. See which topics come up repeatedly and which answers are missing.

Spot FAQ patterns and documentation gaps

If five reps asked about the same feature limitation this week, that’s a signal. Product spots the pattern without waiting for anecdotal feedback.

Update docs and Edgebric re-indexes automatically

Product updates the spec or adds a new FAQ document. Edgebric picks up the changes and sales gets better answers immediately.

Close the feedback loop between field and product. Sales questions surface gaps. Product fills them. Edgebric keeps everyone current.

Getting started

Up and running in three steps

No IT department required. If you can install an app, you can set up Edgebric for your sales and product teams.

1. Install the app

Download, drag to Applications, double-click. Edgebric downloads and manages the AI for you automatically. No technical setup.

2. Add your data

Connect Google Drive, OneDrive, or other storage — Edgebric syncs automatically. You can also drag and drop files directly.

3. Ask your AI anything

Ask questions, get answers with citations. Summarize files, search across everything — all powered by AI running on your own hardware.
