Compliance Review

Scenario: Compliance officer reviewing new regulatory requirements

Upload regulatory documents, guidance memos, and rule updates

Drag and drop into a “Regulatory” data source. Edgebric processes dense legal text and indexes it for natural-language search.

“Summarize the new reporting requirements from the Q3 guidance”

Edgebric extracts the key points with page references so you can verify each requirement against the original text.

Cross-reference against existing compliance checklists

Ask follow-up questions to compare new requirements against your existing policies. Identify gaps before they become violations.

Stay current on regulations without sending them to third-party servers. Regulatory documents remain on your hardware.
