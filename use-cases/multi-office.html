Cross-department Search

Scenario: Company with offices in New York (HR), Chicago (Finance), and Los Angeles (Clients)

Each office runs Edgebric with its own data

New York holds HR policies and legal documents. Chicago holds financial reports and budgets. Los Angeles holds client contracts and project files.

“Are there compliance issues with our new campaign?”

The query fans out to all three offices simultaneously. Each office searches its own data locally—HR checks policy docs, Finance checks regulatory filings, LA checks client agreements.

Answers come back with citations from each office

Only short answer snippets travel back over the network. No files ever leave the building they’re stored in. Every answer cites its source.

Your data stays on your hardware. Only questions and answers travel between offices.

Department Isolation

Scenario: Legal department with sensitive documents alongside other departments

Legal keeps sensitive data on their own machine

Privileged communications, contracts under review, and pending litigation files live exclusively on Legal’s Edgebric node. No copies exist anywhere else.

Other departments can query but never download

Sales asks “Does our standard contract cover data residency in the EU?” and gets an answer. But they never see the contract itself—only the relevant excerpt.

Legal can revoke access at any time

If a matter becomes sensitive, Legal disconnects their node from the mesh with one click. No files to recall, no permissions to audit across servers.

Isolation enforced by physics, not just permissions. Vault Mode adds another layer—encrypted data sources that are completely invisible to the mesh.

Automatic Discovery

Scenario: Setting up Edgebric across multiple locations

Install Edgebric on each machine

Download, install, and add your local data. Each machine is independent and fully functional on its own.

Put machines on the same network—they find each other

Zero configuration. No IP addresses to type. No DNS entries to create. Edgebric uses automatic discovery to locate other nodes on the network.

Remote offices? Connect over your existing VPN

If your offices already share a VPN, Edgebric nodes discover each other across it. Still your infrastructure, still no third parties.

No IP addresses to configure. No central server to maintain.

Graceful Degradation

Scenario: One office loses connectivity during a workday

Chicago office goes offline

Network outage, power issue, or planned maintenance. The Chicago Edgebric node becomes unreachable.

Queries to remaining offices still work

New York and Los Angeles continue answering questions normally. Chicago’s data sources are marked as unavailable in results—you know what you’re missing.

Chicago comes back—auto-reconnects

When the office is back online, its node rejoins the mesh automatically. No manual intervention. No data was lost because nothing was ever replicated.

The network adapts. No single point of failure.
