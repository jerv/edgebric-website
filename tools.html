How it works

You ask. The AI figures out the rest.

You don’t pick tools manually. The AI reads your question, decides which tools to use, runs them, and synthesizes the results into a single answer.

1. Ask a question

“Compare the old and new vendor agreements and check if the liability cap matches our policy.”

2. AI picks the right tools

It runs compare_documents on both files, then cite_check against your internal policies—all on its own.

3. You get a complete answer

A cited response with what changed, what matches, and what doesn’t. A collapsible panel shows exactly which tools ran.

Capabilities

What the tools cover

Search & discovery

Hybrid vector + keyword search across all your knowledge bases. Find related documents. Get source overviews. List everything you have access to.

Analysis

Compare two documents side by side. Fact-check claims against your data with cited evidence. Surface connections you didn’t know existed.

Management

Create knowledge bases, upload documents, delete files, and organize your data—all through natural language mid-conversation.

Personal vault

Save summaries, notes, or findings to your encrypted personal vault during a conversation. Only you can see vault data.

Web access

Search the internet via DuckDuckGo and read any URL. No API keys. Automatically disabled in Vault Mode to keep private data isolated.

Multi-tool chaining

The AI can chain up to 5 rounds of tool calls per question—searching your data, checking the web, comparing results, and saving a summary, all in one go.
