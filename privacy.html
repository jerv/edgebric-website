Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 7, 2026

The Short Version

Edgebric does not collect, store, or transmit your data. Period. The software runs entirely on your hardware. There are no Edgebric servers receiving your information. This policy exists to explain exactly what that means in practice.

1. Data Collection

Edgebric collects no data from you. Specifically:

  • No telemetry or usage analytics
  • No crash reports sent to us
  • No personal information collected
  • No account creation required (in solo mode)
  • No fingerprinting or device identification

2. Cookies & Local Storage

Edgebric does not use tracking cookies. The application uses local session cookies solely to maintain your login session within the app on your own machine. No cookie data is transmitted to Edgebric or any third party.

3. Third-Party Tracking

Edgebric includes no third-party tracking of any kind. No Google Analytics, no Meta Pixel, no Hotjar, no Sentry, no Mixpanel—nothing. The software makes no network requests to advertising or analytics services.

4. Where Your Data Lives

All data processed by Edgebric—your documents, embeddings, conversations, search history, and user settings—is stored locally on your hardware. Edgebric does not operate cloud infrastructure. There is no “Edgebric cloud.” Your data never leaves your machine unless you explicitly choose to share it.

5. Cloud Integrations

Edgebric can connect to third-party cloud services (Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox, etc.) at your direction. When you connect a cloud source:

  • Documents are pulled to your local machine for processing
  • Edgebric does not store your documents on any external server
  • OAuth tokens for cloud services are stored locally on your machine in encrypted form
  • You can disconnect cloud sources and delete local copies at any time

Your use of third-party services is governed by their respective privacy policies.

6. Network Requests

The only network request Edgebric makes without explicit user action is checking for software updates via the GitHub Releases API. This check sends a standard HTTP request to GitHub’s public API and does not transmit any personal data, usage data, or device identifiers.

All other network activity (cloud source syncing, web search via tools, etc.) happens only when you explicitly initiate it.

7. AI Processing

Edgebric runs AI models locally on your hardware using llama.cpp. Your documents and queries are processed on your machine. Nothing is sent to OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, or any other AI provider. Your data is never used to train any model.

8. Authentication

In multi-user mode, Edgebric supports OIDC/SSO authentication (Google, Microsoft). Authentication tokens are handled via your identity provider and stored locally. Edgebric does not maintain a user database on external servers.

9. Children’s Privacy

Edgebric does not collect data from anyone, including children. Since no data is collected or transmitted, there is no applicable concern under COPPA or similar regulations.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Our core commitment—no data collection, no tracking, everything local—will not change.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at support@edgebric.com.
