The reality

Most AI services require sending your data to someone else’s servers.

Contracts, medical records, tax returns, employee files—the more sensitive the data, the more useful AI would be. But you can’t use cloud AI for any of it.

You shouldn’t have to choose between AI and privacy.

Edgebric runs on your hardware. Your data never leaves your network. You get the power of AI without giving up control.

Getting started

Set up in minutes. No IT department required.

If you can install an app, you can set up Edgebric. It runs on everyday consumer hardware—a laptop, a desktop, or a dedicated machine for your office.

1. Install the app

Download, drag to Applications, double-click. Edgebric downloads and manages the AI for you automatically. No technical setup.

2. Add your data

Connect Google Drive, OneDrive, or other storage—Edgebric syncs automatically. You can also drag and drop files directly.

+ more models

3. Ask your AI anything

Ask questions, get answers with citations. The AI can also use built-in tools—comparing documents, fact-checking claims, searching the web, and more—all on its own.

What you need

No GPUs. No server rack. Just a Mac.

Edgebric is designed for Apple silicon—the same chips in every modern Mac. No fancy hardware, no datacenter, no IT department.

Personal use

Your existing Mac laptop or desktop

Any Mac with Apple silicon and at least 16 GB of memory. MacBook Air, MacBook Pro, iMac, Mac Mini—if it was made in the last few years, it runs Edgebric.

Team use

A compact desktop — starting at $599

A small, silent desktop sits in your office. Plug it in, install Edgebric, and your team connects through their browser. No GPU required—Apple silicon handles everything.

Why Apple silicon? Modern Macs have unified memory that handles AI workloads natively—no discrete GPU needed. That’s what makes machines starting at $599 capable of running AI models up to the task.
